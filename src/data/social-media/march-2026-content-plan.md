## 1. PLATFORM STRATEGY

### YouTube (Long-form)
- **Purpose**: Authority building, SEO, deep storytelling. YouTube is your discovery engine -- people searching "financial literacy Central Asia" or "how to start a nonprofit" will find you here.
- **Frequency**: 1 video per week (4 total in March)
- **Format**: 8-15 minute videos recorded in Descript with teleprompter, green screen backgrounds, and AI B-roll. Kyrgyz-dubbed versions created via Descript audio dubbing.
- **Content mix**: Founder story (1), Educational deep-dive (1), Problem/mission awareness (1), Impact/vision (1)

### YouTube Shorts
- **Purpose**: Reach and discovery. Shorts feed the algorithm and funnel viewers to long-form.
- **Frequency**: 3-4 per week (14 total in March)
- **Format**: 30-60 second vertical videos. Hook in first 2 seconds, one clear idea, strong CTA.
- **Content mix**: Quick tips (5), Myth-busting (3), Story snippets (3), Behind-the-scenes (3)

### Instagram
- **Purpose**: Visual storytelling, community building, and donor/volunteer engagement. Instagram is where you build emotional connection.
- **Frequency**: 4-5 posts per week (18 total in March), plus 3-5 Stories per week
- **Format mix**: Carousels (6), Reels cross-posted from Shorts (8), Single-image posts (4)
- **Content mix**: Educational carousels (4), Story-driven posts (4), Mission/CTA posts (4), Reels (6)

### Facebook Page
- **Purpose**: Donor communication, community updates, event promotion. Facebook skews older and is where many nonprofit donors spend time.
- **Frequency**: 3-4 posts per week (14 total in March)
- **Format mix**: Link posts driving to blog (5), Video posts (4), Image posts (3), Text-heavy story posts (2)

### Facebook Group (BBB Community)
- **Purpose**: Deeper engagement with supporters, volunteers, and partners. This is where conversation happens.
- **Frequency**: 2-3 posts per week (10 total in March)
- **Format mix**: Discussion prompts (4), Behind-the-scenes updates (3), Resource shares (3)

### LinkedIn
- **Purpose**: Professional credibility, partnership development, corporate donor outreach. LinkedIn is where you reach people who write checks and make partnership decisions.
- **Frequency**: 3 posts per week (12 total in March)
- **Format mix**: Thought leadership text posts (5), Article shares from blog (4), Milestone/impact posts (3)
